About the fest

In the old story, Sanjeevani was the herb that brought the fallen back to their feet — carried across mountains when nothing else could help. This year, we're borrowing that spirit: a fest built to revive the campus with debate, performance, strategy and a little bit of chaos, all in one place.

Seven events.
One weekend.

Pick one or join them all — registration covers every event you want to be part of.

01
Mock Interview
Individual
A real-format interview round with working professionals as panelists. Walk in with your resume, walk out with honest feedback.
02
BioQuiz
Team
A rapid-fire quiz on biology, life sciences and the science headlines of the year. Teams battle it out across written and buzzer rounds.
03
Traitor — The Imposter Game
Group
One traitor hides among the group, sabotaging missions in secret. Everyone else has one job: find them before it's too late.
04
Talent Show
Individual or Group
Singing, dancing, or any art form you can bring to a stage. Open floor, open format — show us what you've got.
05
Photography
Individual
Capture the fest as it happens, or submit a themed portfolio shot beforehand. Judged on composition, story and originality.
06
Battle Zone
Squad · BGMI / Free Fire
Squad up and drop in. A bracketed BGMI and Free Fire tournament running across knockout rounds to a final showdown.
07
Lok Sabha — Political Debate
Individual or Team
A parliamentary-style debate on the issues shaping the country today. Argue your bench, defend your motion, win the house.
